abstract: Hierarchical problem abstraction, when applicable, may offer exponential
  reductions in computational complexity. Previous work on coarse-to-fine dynamic
  programming (CFDP) has demonstrated this possibility using state abstraction to
  speed up the Viterbi algorithm. In this paper, we show how to apply temporal abstraction
  to the Viterbi problem. Our algorithm uses bounds derived from analysis of coarse
  timescales to prune large parts of the state trellis at finer timescales. We demonstrate
  improvements of several orders of magnitude over the standard Viterbi algorithm,
  as well as significant speedups over CFDP, for problems whose state variables evolve
  at widely differing rates.
