abstract: 'We consider filtering for a continuous-time, or asynchronous, stochastic
  system where the full distribution over states is too large to be stored or calculated.
  We assume that the rate matrix of the system can be compactly represented and that
  the belief distribution is to be approximated as a product of marginals. The essential
  computation is the matrix exponential. We look at two different methods for its
  computation: ODE integration and uniformization of the Taylor expansion. For both
  we consider approximations in which only a factored belief state is maintained.
  For factored uniformization we demonstrate that the KL-divergence of the filtering
  is bounded. Our experimental results confirm our factored uniformization performs
  better than previously suggested uniformization methods and the mean field algorithm.'
