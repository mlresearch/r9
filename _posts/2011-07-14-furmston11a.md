abstract: Markov control algorithms that perform smooth, non-greedy updates of the
  policy have been shown to be very general and versatile, with policy gradient and
  Expectation Maximisation algorithms being particularly popular. For these algorithms,
  marginal inference of the reward weighted trajectory distribution is required to
  perform policy updates. We discuss a new exact inference algorithm for these marginals
  in the finite horizon case that is more efficient than the standard approach based
  on classical forward-backward recursions. We also provide a principled extension
  to infinite horizon Markov Decision Problems that explicitly accounts for an infinite
  horizon. This extension provides a novel algorithm for both policy gradients and
  Expectation Maximisation in infinite horizon problems.
