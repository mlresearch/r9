abstract: In this paper, we derive a novel probabilistic model of boosting as a Product
  of Experts. We re-derive the boosting algorithm as a greedy incremental model selection
  procedure which ensures that addition of new experts to the ensemble does not decrease
  the likelihood of the data. These learning rules lead to a generic boosting algorithm
  - POE- Boost which turns out to be similar to the AdaBoost algorithm under certain
  assumptions on the expert probabilities. The paper then extends the POEBoost algorithm
  to POEBoost.CS which handles hypothesis that produce probabilistic predictions.
  This new algorithm is shown to have better generalization performance compared to
  other state of the art algorithms.
