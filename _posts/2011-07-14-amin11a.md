abstract: We introduce a rich class of graphical models for multi-armed bandit problems
  that permit both the state or context space and the action space to be very large,
  yet succinctly specify the payoffs for any context-action pair. Our main result
  is an algorithm for such models whose regret is bounded by the number of parameters
  and whose running time depends only on the treewidth of the graph substructure induced
  by the action space.
