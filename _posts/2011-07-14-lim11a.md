abstract: We present theoretical results in terms of lower and upper bounds on the
  query complexity of noisy search with comparative feedback. In this search model,
  the noise in the feedback depends on the distance between query points and the search
  target. Consequently, the error probability in the feedback is not fixed but varies
  for the queries posed by the search algorithm. Our results show that a target out
  of n items can be found in O(log n) queries. We also show the surprising result
  that for k possible answers per query, the speedup is not log k (as for k-ary search)
  but only log log k in some cases.
