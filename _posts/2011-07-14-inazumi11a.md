abstract: Discovering causal relations among observed variables in a given data set
  is a main topic in studies of statistics and artificial intelligence. Recently,
  some techniques to discover an identifiable causal structure have been explored
  based on non-Gaussianity of the observed data distribution. However, most of these
  are limited to continuous data. In this paper, we present a novel causal model for
  binary data and propose a new approach to derive an identifiable causal structure
  governing the data based on skew Bernoulli distributions of external noise. Experimental
  evaluation shows excellent performance for both artificial and real world data sets.
