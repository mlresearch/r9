abstract: We present a simple, yet effective, approach to Semi-Supervised Learning.
  Our approach is based on estimating density-based distances (DBD) using a shortest
  path calculation on a graph. These Graph-DBD estimates can then be used in any distance-based
  supervised learning method, such as Nearest Neighbor methods and SVMs with RBF kernels.
  In order to apply the method to very large data sets, we also present a novel algorithm
  which integrates nearest neighbor computations into the shortest path search and
  can find exact shortest paths even in extremely large dense graphs. Significant
  runtime improvement over the commonly used Laplacian regularization method is then
  shown on a large scale dataset.
