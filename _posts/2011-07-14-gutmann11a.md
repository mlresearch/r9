abstract: We show that the Bregman divergence provides a rich framework to estimate
  unnormalized statistical models for continuous or discrete random variables, that
  is, models which do not integrate or sum to one, respectively. We prove that recent
  estimation methods such as noise-contrastive estimation, ratio matching, and score
  matching belong to the proposed framework, and explain their interconnection based
  on supervised learning. Further, we discuss the role of boosting in unsupervised
  learning.
