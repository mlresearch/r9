abstract: Fisher score is one of the most widely used supervised feature selection
  methods. However, it selects each feature independently according to their scores
  under the Fisher criterion, which leads to a suboptimal subset of features. In this
  paper, we present a generalized Fisher score to jointly select features. It aims
  at finding an subset of features, which maximize the lower bound of traditional
  Fisher score. The resulting feature selection problem is a mixed integer programming,
  which can be reformulated as a quadratically constrained linear programming (QCLP).
  It is solved by cutting plane algorithm, in each iteration of which a multiple kernel
  learning problem is solved alternatively by multivariate ridge regression and projected
  gradient descent. Experiments on benchmark data sets indicate that the proposed
  method outperforms Fisher score as well as many other state-of-the-art feature selection
  methods.
