abstract: The problem of learning the structure of Bayesian networks from complete
  discrete data with a limit on parent set size is considered. Learning is cast explicitly
  as an optimisation problem where the goal is to find a BN structure which maximises
  log marginal likelihood (BDe score). Integer programming, specifically the SCIP
  framework, is used to solve this optimisation problem. Acyclicity constraints are
  added to the integer program (IP) during solving in the form of cutting planes.
  Finding good cutting planes is the key to the success of the approach -the search
  for such cutting planes is effected using a sub-IP. Results show that this is a
  particularly fast method for exact BN learning.
