abstract: We propose a method called EDML for learning MAP parameters in binary Bayesian
  networks under incomplete data. The method assumes Beta priors and can be used to
  learn maximum likelihood parameters when the priors are uninformative. EDML exhibits
  interesting behaviors, especially when compared to EM. We introduce EDML, explain
  its origin, and study some of its properties both analytically and empirically.
