abstract: Standard maximum likelihood estimation cannot be applied to discrete energy-based
  models in the general case because the computation of exact model probabilities
  is intractable. Recent research has seen the proposal of several new estimators
  designed specifically to overcome this intractability, but virtually nothing is
  known about their theoretical properties. In this paper, we present a generalized
  estimator that unifies many of the classical and recently proposed estimators. We
  use results from the standard asymptotic theory for M-estimators to derive a generic
  expression for the asymptotic covariance matrix of our generalized estimator. We
  apply these results to study the relative statistical efficiency of classical pseudolikelihood
  and the recently-proposed ratio matching estimator.
