abstract: We present a new Markov chain Monte Carlo method for estimating posterior
  probabilities of structural features in Bayesian networks. The method draws samples
  from the posterior distribution of partial orders on the nodes; for each sampled
  partial order, the conditional probabilities of interest are computed exactly. We
  give both analytical and empirical results that suggest the superiority of the new
  method compared to previous methods, which sample either directed acyclic graphs
  or linear orders on the nodes.
