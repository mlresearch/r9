abstract: In this paper, we develop a qualitative theory of influence diagrams that
  can be used to model and solve sequential decision making tasks when only qualitative
  (or imprecise) information is available. Our approach is based on an order-of-magnitude
  approximation of both probabilities and utilities and allows for specifying partially
  ordered preferences via sets of utility values. We also propose a dedicated variable
  elimination algorithm that can be applied for solving order-of-magnitude influence
  diagrams.
