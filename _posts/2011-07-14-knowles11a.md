abstract: 'We introduce the Pitman Yor Diffusion Tree (PYDT) for hierarchical clustering,
  a generalization of the Dirichlet Diffusion Tree (Neal, 2001) which removes the
  restriction to binary branching structure. The generative process is described and
  shown to result in an exchangeable distribution over data points. We prove some
  theoretical properties of the model and then present two inference methods: a collapsed
  MCMC sampler which allows us to model uncertainty over tree structures, and a computationally
  efficient greedy Bayesian EM search algorithm. Both algorithms use message passing
  on the tree structure. The utility of the model and algorithms is demonstrated on
  synthetic and real world data, both continuous and binary.'
