abstract: Marginal MAP problems are notoriously difficult tasks for graphical models.
  We derive a general variational framework for solving marginal MAP problems, in
  which we apply analogues of the Bethe, tree-reweighted, and mean field approximations.
  We then derive a "mixed" message passing algorithm and a convergent alternative
  using CCCP to solve the BP-type approximations. Theoretically, we give conditions
  under which the decoded solution is a global or local optimum, and obtain novel
  upper bounds on solutions. Experimentally we demonstrate that our algorithms outperform
  related approaches. We also show that EM and variational EM comprise a special case
  of our framework.
